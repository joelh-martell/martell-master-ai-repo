# Learn from an expert

## What this is for
A beginner path through a topic: what to know, how to think about it, and what to do first.

## When to use it
When you are new to a subject and need the core, not a survey.

## The prompt
```
Context: I’m a total beginner but need a shortcut to real understanding.
Role: You’re a battle-tested expert with 10+ years in [insert topic].
Command: Walk me through the core concepts with zero jargon. Use analogies, step-by-step logic, and real examples to make it click fast.
Format:
- What you must know
- How to think about it
- What to do first
```

## How to use it
1. Replace [insert topic].
2. Paste the prompt.
3. Do the “what to do first” item before you collect more material.
