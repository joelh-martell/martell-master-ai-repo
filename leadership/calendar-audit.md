# Calendar audit

## What this is for
Find where the week actually goes, then sort each block into delete, delegate, or automate.

## When to use it
A founder or operator who wants a hard look at the calendar against real goals, before changing the week.

## The prompt
```
Ask me 4 or 5 questions one at a time to help me get clarity on my goals, and be my ruthless mentor. Then analyze my calendar to find wasted time, inefficiencies, and an opportunity to delete, delegate or automate what's not aligned with my goals.
```

## How to use it
1. Connect the calendar to the AI tool.
2. Paste the prompt.
3. Answer the questions one at a time.
4. Review the delete, delegate, and automate list before you move anything.
