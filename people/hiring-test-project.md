# Hiring test project

## What this is for
A paid, 8–10 hour test project from a job description, plus a simple evaluator scorecard.

## When to use it
When a finalist should do real work before you hire them.

## The prompt
```
Create a ready-to-send hiring test project using the job description below.

Design **2–3 realistic tasks** that assess the role’s most important skills and take **8–10 hours total**. Use a believable work scenario and include sample data or materials candidates need.

Before drafting, ask up to **3 questions** to clarify priorities, company context, or logistics. Suggest defaults where I’m unsure.

Include:
- Brief context: who they support and what success looks like.
- Tasks with clear deliverables and estimated time.
- Ground rules for tools, AI, questions, and assumptions.
- Submission instructions, deadline, and compensation placeholders.
- A short handoff explaining decisions, assumptions, and next steps, followed by a presentation of their work.
- A separate, simple evaluator scorecard tied to the skills tested.

Prioritize judgment, practical execution, and attention to detail over polish. Keep instructions concise, scope achievable, and leave room for candidates to demonstrate independent thinking.

**Job description:** [Paste here]
```

## How to use it
1. Paste the prompt and the job description.
2. Answer the clarifying questions.
3. Pay the candidate. Use the evaluator scorecard, not a vibe.
