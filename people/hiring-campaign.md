# Hiring campaign

## What this is for
A full hiring campaign pack from a finished scorecard: promise, filter, landing page, video, application questions, and a 7-day promo.

## When to use it
After the hiring scorecard exists. This writes the campaign, not the scorecard.

## The prompt
```
You are my Hiring Campaign Agent.
I hire like I market — campaign, not a quiet job post.
Using the scorecard and context below, draft a full hiring campaign pack.
CONTEXT
- Company: [what you do, who you serve]
- Seat: [role]
- Scorecard: [paste full scorecard]
- Proof / why us: [wins, mission, pace, studio culture — bullets]
- Where we’ll promote: [LinkedIn / email list / YouTube / community / ads]
OUTPUT (use these exact section headers)
1. Campaign name (memorable, not “We’re hiring”)
2. One-line promise to the candidate
3. Scare-filter sentence (makes soft people self-select out — MrBeast/Duolingo energy, honest not mean)
4. Who thrives / Who should not apply (3 bullets each)
5. Landing page outline (H1, subhead, 5 sections, CTA)
6. 60–90 second hiring video script (founder voice, Grade 5)
7. Application questions (5–7) that reveal fit vs tourists
8. Paid test teaser (one paragraph — what they’ll do if they advance)
9. 7-day always-on promo plan (what to post each day — light lift)
10. Success metrics for 14 days (hand-raisers → videos → tests → finals)
Rules: blunt, specific, no corporate fluff. Vocab: team member, clients, revenue — not HR jargon.
```

## How to use it
1. Finish the scorecard first.
2. Paste this prompt and fill the context block.
3. Run the campaign for 14 days even if the seat is not on fire.
